# CHIPS reconciliation with decimals restored: decimal-cols full-minmax vs published spreadsheet

Generated by `scripts/reconcile_chips.py` and `scripts/divergence_detail.py`
run with `--data-file "(Decimal cols) SIDE 2026 - Rohan - Absolute.csv"` (and
output redirected here). The dashboard engine applies the published-matching
conventions: indicator scores rounded to 1 decimal place (0–100 scale), final
CHIPS rounded to 2 decimal places, and the 1-of-2 missing-data exception (a
2-component group with only 1 present is kept, reweighting the survivor).

This is the same reconciliation as in `min-max mismatch investigation/`, but
with the raw data columns stored at full precision (the earlier run used the
rounded `SIDE 2026 - Rohan - Absolute.csv`).

## What the decimals change

With decimal precision the dashboard CHIPS move very little: only 45 of 71
countries shift at all, and the largest single movement is 0.03 points. The
reconciliation outcome is therefore nearly the same as the rounded run, except
the *type* of residual mismatch changes:

- **Switzerland** and **Indonesia** drop out of the INDICATOR bucket. Their
  earlier mismatch (Public trust score ~0.5 pts for Switzerland; Safety and
  security ~0.6 pts for Indonesia) disappears once decimals restore the exact
  published indicator values (dashboard now 12.95 vs published 12.9 for Public
  trust; 55.95 vs 56.0 for Safety and security). They now appear only as
  AGGREGATION / WEIGHTS residual.
- **Russian Federation** crosses the 0.1-point boundary (0.09 → 0.10) purely
  because the decimals nudge its dashboard score from 20.28 to 20.27; the gap is
  still a borderline INNOVATE · AI aggregation residual, not a data problem.
- Distribution moves from 62 ROUNDING-ONLY / 6 AGG / 3 INDICATOR to 61 / 9 / 1.

## Root-cause classification

### 1. AGGREGATION / WEIGHTS (9)
Bangladesh (1.19), Croatia (0.58), Switzerland (0.29), Chile (0.20), Indonesia
(0.13), Kenya (0.12), Hungary (0.12), Qatar (0.11), Russian Federation (0.10).
Indicators and sub-pillars reconcile, but the aggregate differs. Driven by the
INNOVATE · AI sub-pillar, which the dashboard aggregates through two internal
groups (research pair ½ + investment/commercial ½) while the published
spreadsheet uses a flat weighting. Bangladesh's larger residual also reflects
the published spreadsheet not linking the sustainability patent indicator into
its score (fixed in the dashboard).

### 2. INDICATOR (data-version / minmax bound) (1)
- Italy fixed broadband: dashboard raw=46 → 73.0; published=11.9 (implies raw ≈
  132). This is the published report's mistyped price; the dashboard keeps the
  corrected raw value of 46.

### 3. ROUNDING-ONLY (61)
Countries within 0.1 points; the remaining gap is sub-0.1 rounding/structural
noise below the reconciliation threshold.
