# CHIPS reconciliation: Relative scores (dashboard full-minmax vs published)

Generated by `scripts/reconcile_chips.py` and `scripts/divergence_detail.py` run
against the **Relative** base scores:

- Published source: `data/SIDE 2026 - AI augmented Relative Index 2026.csv` (CHIPS score line 96).
- Dashboard source: `data/SIDE 2026 - Relative.csv` (the dashboard's new default
  base dataset), full-range min-max, Relative columns mapped onto the canonical
  CHIPS indicator set.

## Summary

- 71 countries reconciled; **42 match to within 0.1 points** (3 exactly).
- 28 countries diverge by more than 0.1 points (largest: Italy 1.71, Switzerland 1.65).
- 2 countries (Dominican Republic, Kuwait) have no published CHIPS score but do
  on the dashboard — they are counted as diverging.

| Root cause | Countries |
|---|---|
| ROUNDING-ONLY | 43 |
| INDICATOR (data-version / indicator bound) | 28 |

## What the divergence is driven by

Almost every flagged country traces back to **one PROTECT · Risk of Attack
indicator: Ransomware attacks (30-day average)**:

- **25 of the 28 flagged countries** show a large dashboard-vs-published gap on
  this single indicator (rows in `divergences_detail.csv` labelled
  "Ransomware attacks 30 day average", e.g. Italy 75.8 vs 100.0, Switzerland
  36.3 vs 0.0, Russian Federation 57.1 vs 100.0).
- Cause: the Relative CSV's ransomware column
  (`Ransomware attacks detected per month as a % of internet users`) is
  **effectively constant** — only 4 of 71 countries are non-zero — so the
  dashboard's full-range min-max collapses it (everyone near 0 / near the best
  score) and cannot reproduce the published Relative index's ransomware scores,
  which carry real spread. The published Relative ransomware row does not match
  this column (rank correlation ≈ 0.36); it tracks the *absolute* ransomware
  figures better (≈ 0.61), i.e. the published sheet appears to score Ransomware
  attacks from data that is not present in the Relative CSV. This inflates the
  dashboard's PROTECT pillar vs published for those countries.

Secondary, smaller contributors:
- **% of population that are using AI** (CONNECT · AI): 13 countries, each a
  small 0.5–1.2-point indicator gap (e.g. Finland 37.3 vs 37.9) — a rounding /
  indicator-bound mismatch, not a wholesale data error.
- **Italy** — Fixed broadband price (published 11.9, dashboard 97.0) and the AI
  % gap; this is the same known published broadband-data issue as in the
  Absolute reconciliation.
- **South Africa / Uzbekistan** — Renewable energy share and VC investment
  indicators are present in the published sheet but **missing (NaN) in the
  dashboard Relative data** for those countries, so their SUSTAINABILITY pillar
  diverges.
- **Switzerland** — an INNOVATE · AI sub-pillar weighting difference (31.8 vs
  18.4) on top of the ransomware / AI-users gaps.

## Comparison with the Absolute reconciliation

| | Absolute (decimals + AI keep-if-any) | Relative |
|---|---|---|
| within 0.1 pts | 68 / 71 | 42 / 71 |
| exact matches | 42 | 3 |

The Relative dashboard is much further from the published Relative index than
the Absolute dashboard was from the published Absolute index. The gap is not a
scaling artifact: it is almost entirely explained by the Ransomware-attacks
indicator whose Relative input column is degenerate, plus a handful of
data-version differences (Italy broadband, missing renewables/VC cells, small
%‑AI‑users bound drift).
