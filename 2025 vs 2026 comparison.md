## Summary

- 24 clean matches, 5 partial matches, 44 with no 2025 Global counterpart (including the stray Count column and 2 Relative-only extras).
- Almost all matches come from the Global Connect, Harness and Protect indicator sets. The Innovate/Other-Emerging-Technology, Sustainability and AI sub-pillar indicators are essentially new in 2026 — 2025 has no Global equivalent.
- Matching is conceptual, not literal: 2026 headers are curated canonical names while 2025 names are raw source strings, so units often differ (absolute vs %, PPP$ vs % GNI, count vs per-100).

## Open questions

Two things worth confirming before this becomes an implementation plan:

2. The AI Tortoise/Vibrancy indicators (62, 63, 67, 68, 71) are from a different source than anything in 2025 — do you want those treated as partial matches or as "no match"?
