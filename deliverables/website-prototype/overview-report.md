## Notes

- `field.median`, `.min` and `.max` are computed from that method's 71 scores; the median of an odd count is an observed score.
- `top15` selects ranks 1-15 within its method; a tie at 15 would carry the tied rows and show up in `top15MaxRank`.
- Ranks are emitted as integers (`68`, not `68.0`); `chips.json` stores them as floats. The comparison is numeric.
- `composition` is the top 10 by that method's overall rank.
- `coverage.coverage` is the engine's weight-weighted data coverage; `indicatorsPresent`/`indicatorsTotal` are the raw leaf counts. Coverage itself is method-independent, but the score and rank carried beside it are not.
- No file under `data/SIDE *.csv` is read for writing, and none is modified. Every number comes from `core/chips.py` through `core/loader.py`.
